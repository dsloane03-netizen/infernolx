290WBeam moving head

Tight, bright beam moving head that cuts a long, sharp shaft of light through haze and smoke. It has a 14-colour wheel, 14 gobo patterns and three prisms for splitting the beam into a fan or a honeycomb of light, and a colour LED ring around the lens adds a glow even when the beam is off. Great over dance floors, in sheds and warehouses, and in haze outdoors.
